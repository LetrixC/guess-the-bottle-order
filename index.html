CRACK THE SECRET ORDER OF 4 BOTTLES IN 10 TURNS OR LESS

HOW TO PLAY

  • 4 bottles are hidden in a secret order. 6 colours, no repeats.
  • Pick a bottle for each slot, then press CONFIRM.
  • ● PERFECT = right colour, right slot.
  • ○ COLOUR ONLY = right colour, wrong slot.
  • Crack the code in 10 turns or fewer.
  • DUO: take turns on one device. No talking!

DUO RULES

  • You share one secret code and one turn counter.
  • Players take turns: P1, P2, P1, P2...
  • Pass the device between turns. NO TALKING, NO HINTS!
  • Read the clues your teammate left in the log.
  • The result card shows both names and who guessed how often.
