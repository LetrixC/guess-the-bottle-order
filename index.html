HOW TO PLAY

  • 4 bottles are hidden in a secret order. No colour repeats.
  • Pick a bottle for each place, then press CONFIRM.
  • ● PERFECT = right colour, right place.
  • ○ COLOUR ONLY = right colour, wrong place.
  • Crack the code in 10 turns or fewer.
  • DUO: create an online room and share the 4-digit code, or play on one device. Take turns. No talking!
